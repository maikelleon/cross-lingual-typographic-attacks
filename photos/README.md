`scripts/make_figures.py` crops a square around the dog, with its left edge at 23.9% of the width,
its top edge at 2.9% of the height, and a side equal to 69.9% of the height, resizes the square to
224 × 224 pixels, and writes each word on it exactly as on the stimulus photographs. Because the
crop is defined in proportions, the original file yields the same region. The badge on the dog's
collar lies under the written word in every panel, and the use of the photograph implies no
endorsement by the FBI.
